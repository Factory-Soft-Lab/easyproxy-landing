To help us help you faster

When you report a problem, include what you know of this:

  • Device model and system version (iOS or Android).
  • App version: it is under More → About.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • If the app closed by itself, the report under More → Diagnostics: you can share it from there.

Do not send us captured traffic without reviewing it. A HAR or a copied request can carry your apps' tokens, cookies or passwords. If needed, trim or redact those values first. We will never ask you for a password.

Frequently asked questions

Why does {{ app }} need a VPN?

Because it is the only way iOS and Android let one app see the traffic of others. It is a local VPN: traffic does not go through any server of ours, it goes straight to where it was headed. It is explained in the privacy policy.

I see requests but not the content of HTTPS ones.

Reading HTTPS needs two things: installing and trusting the app's certificate, and adding the domain under More → SSL Proxying. Only what is on that list is decrypted; without {{ pro }}, up to {{ free.ssl_domains }} domains.

An app stops loading when I turn capture on.

It is almost always an app that does not accept user-installed certificates (certificate pinning) and refuses to connect when its traffic is decrypted. Remove its domain from SSL Proxying: you will still see it, without the content. On Android, most third-party apps also do not trust user certificates since Android 7; your own apps can, through their network_security_config.

How do I remove the certificate?

Uninstalling the app does not remove it. On iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → {{ app }} → Remove Profile. On Android: Settings → Security & privacy → More security & privacy → Encryption & credentials → Trusted credentials → User. Menu names may vary by manufacturer.

What does the free version include?

Capturing, the inspector and HAR export, with no limits. Without {{ pro }}, the first {{ free.rules_per_tool }} rules of each tool are applied, {{ free.ssl_domains }} domains are decrypted and {{ free.pro_only_tools }} is not included. The details are in the terms.

How does the free trial work?

The renewing plans may start with {{ free.trial_days }} free days, once per store account. If you do not cancel before it ends —on the App Store, at least 24 hours before— it converts to paid on the plan you chose. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged and keep Pro until those days are over.

I bought Pro and it does not show up.

Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen, with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. If it still does not appear, write to us with the store and the date of purchase.

How do I cancel the subscription?

From your store account: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

How do I ask for a refund?

The store handles it: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com and Google from your Google Play order history. We cannot issue refunds, but write to us and we will help.

How do I delete everything captured?

Under Settings → Clear captured data, or by uninstalling the app. With no account and no server, that removes everything. The certificate is removed separately (see above).
