The VPN is local: traffic never leaves the device

What it does

  • Every call, in full

    Method, URL, headers, bodies and timings of everything your apps send, with readable JSON and search. Capturing has no limits.

  • HTTPS, only where you say

    A certificate generated on your phone and a list of domains. Anything not on the list passes through unopened.

  • Change the response

    Rewrite a JSON field, pause a request to edit it, block a domain or serve a fake response without touching the server.

  • A worse network, on purpose

    Added delay and limited bandwidth for the requests you pick, to see how your app behaves on the subway before a review tells you.

  • Scripting

    Your own code on every request and response, for what does not fit in a rule. Included in {{ pro }}.

  • Take it where you work

    Export to HAR, copy a request as cURL or share a mock flow with your team.

What leaves the phone and what does not

The traffic you capture never leaves the device. There are no accounts, no server of ours, and the app has no analytics or ads. The certificate key is generated on your phone and stays there.

What leaves is what you choose to take out —a HAR you export, a request you replay— and, if you run it, the speed test against Cloudflare.

It is all spelled out, no fine print, in the privacy policy.
